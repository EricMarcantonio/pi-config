# Substitutions

Every known translation from a reference material to wood stock, with the
dimensional consequence. Add a row whenever a build teaches a new one; the
workbook's deviations table is generated from these rows, so an unrecorded
substitution is a lie of omission.

## Resin panel shed → stick-framed wood (Keter Walnut Pent 97, SKU 1001865367)

| reference | wood build | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 40 mm resin double-wall panel | 11 mm grooved siding + 11 mm OSB + 89 mm 2x4 = **111 mm** | interior clear 2708.9 × 2099.3 → 2566.9 × 1957.3 mm; 5.64 → 5.02 m²; ≈ 11.3 → 10.2 m³ |
| 45 × 45 mm moulded corner post | 3-stud 2x4 corner, 89 × 140 | corner trim detail needed; clerestory band narrows 2698.92 → **2610.92 mm** |
| clerestory band under an 80 mm resin roof | same 300 mm band under a 120 mm wood roof, **dropped 40 mm** | sill strip above the doors shrinks 67 → 27 mm |
| triangular louvre vent, 620 × 300 | 12 × 18 in rectangular gable vent | **shape change**; triangular filler panel needed |
| resin floor + 60 mm plinth | PT joists + 18 mm T&G deck | **196 mm of structure below the datum**; the pad must be excavated/levelled; door opening preserved at 1811 mm |
| resin/steel roof sandwich, 80 mm | 2x4 rafters @ 304.8 + mid purlin + OSB + steel, 120 mm | 2x6 rafters do **not** fit under a fixed envelope with a 300 mm band |
| moulded resin door leaves | 38 × 89 framed leaves + 18 mm exterior plywood skin | hinges must suit outward-swinging doors; 3 hinges per leaf |
| level ground only | compacted gravel / pavers + ground-contact skids | **new requirement** the reference does not have |
| 145 mm plank groove pitch | manufacturer's grooved siding, laid horizontally | pitch becomes the supplier's (cosmetic) |
| ground-contact PT 2x4 joists and sole plate | above-ground PT 2x4 on **ground-contact 4x4 skids** | Home Depot Canada stocks no ground-contact 2x4 at all; the joists and plate are above grade |

## Rules that came out of this build

- **A substitution that changes the diagram must say so.** The band narrowing, the
  vent changing shape, the floor moving below the datum and the roof section all
  change the drawing; the wall thickness and groove pitch do not.
- **Preserve what the reference promises.** The door opening (the machine that has
  to fit through it) and the exterior envelope were held exactly while everything
  else gave. Decide this *before* deriving anything.
- **Prefer a product that already exists over making one.** The whole point is to
  trade "buy a resin shed" for "buy stock a store sells".
- **When a material is unavailable, redesign rather than substitute silently.** The
  ground-contact case is the model: rather than buy above-ground joists and hope,
  the floor detail was changed so only the skids need ground contact — and the
  reasoning is in the row.
